# Build a 13-week cash forecast
Status: public preview. Start from verified cash, dated receivables, payables, payroll, tax, debt, and operating assumptions. Keep expected, upside, and downside scenarios separate. Require all thirteen ordered weekly rows with opening cash, inflows, outflows and ending cash. Reconcile each roll-forward and continuity into the next week; do not label a six-week excerpt a complete thirteen-week forecast. Preserve missing cash or flows as unknown rather than zero, and label assumptions versus booked facts. Produce a weekly table, lowest-cash point, runway risks, and accessible charts. Human owners validate timing and choose actions.
