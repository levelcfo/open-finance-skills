# Build a 13-week cash forecast
Status: public preview. Start from verified cash, dated receivables, payables, payroll, tax, debt, and operating assumptions. Keep expected, upside, and downside scenarios separate. Reconcile each roll-forward and label assumptions versus booked facts. Produce a weekly table, lowest-cash point, runway risks, and accessible charts. Human owners validate timing and choose actions.
