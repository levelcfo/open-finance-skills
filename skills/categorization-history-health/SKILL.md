# Check categorization history
Measure historical coverage, consistency by normalized vendor, overrides, ambiguous vendors, and time drift. Return stable candidates, conflicting history, and records requiring manual policy. Do not create automation from a thin or contradictory sample. A finance professional approves the accounting treatment and rule scope.
