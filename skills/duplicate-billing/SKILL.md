# Detect duplicate billing
Compare invoice number, normalized vendor, amount, date, source document, and payment state. Separate exact duplicates from similar candidates. Never void, delete, or reverse a transaction. Return record identifiers, the matching fields, conflicting fields, dollars at risk, and the documents a reviewer must inspect. Final disposition belongs to a finance professional.
