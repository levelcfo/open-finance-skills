# Review idle cash
Status: public preview. Require account balances, observation dates, operating-cash policy, forecast needs, yield assumption, and source date. Calculate an illustrative opportunity, never guaranteed earnings. Average cash minus a buffer is only a conditional scenario, not eligible funds. Require the lowest forecast unrestricted cash after obligations, dated coverage and restrictions; missing evidence leaves eligibility unknown. Show any surplus under the stated policy separately from approved funds. Return conditional-balance scenarios, annualized and holding-period estimates with explicit time bases, liquidity buffer, concentration considerations, and limitations. A finance professional and authorized signer decide whether funds may move.
