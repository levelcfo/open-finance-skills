# Review margin trends
Require consistent revenue and direct-cost definitions across periods. Recompute gross profit and margin rather than trusting displayed percentages. Flag threshold movements and separate arithmetic from causal explanations. Return period values, percentage-point change, data limitations, and driver questions covering labor, materials, price, callbacks, and mix. Human judgment determines the supported cause.
