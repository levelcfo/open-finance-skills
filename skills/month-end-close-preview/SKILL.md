# Preview month-end close
Require a named entity and month. Check source freshness, account reconciliations, subledger ties, schedules, open exceptions, and reviewer sign-offs. Return ready, blocked, unknown, and not-applicable states with evidence. Never post entries or declare the books closed. The controller or designated reviewer makes the final close decision.
