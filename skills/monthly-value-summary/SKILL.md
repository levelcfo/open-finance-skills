# Summarize monthly finance value
Consume only completed review outputs with their evidence, dates, and limitations. Separate recurring savings, one-time cash release, risk avoided, and non-dollar operational findings. Never add unlike value categories into one inflated number. Return movements, decisions, owners, due dates, and unresolved questions for final CFO review.
