# Review monthly variances
Status: public preview. Require consistent account mappings, periods, actuals, budget, and comparison basis. Calculate dollar and percentage variance, but do not rank tiny denominators by percentage alone. Separate timing, volume, price, mix, classification, and data-quality hypotheses. Return the largest decision-relevant variances and questions. Human review confirms the driver.
