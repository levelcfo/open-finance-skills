# Review recurring subscriptions
Cluster normalized payees with similar amounts and cadence. Identify new charges, price increases, duplicate tools, annual renewals, and subscriptions with uncertain ownership. Never cancel a service. Return cadence evidence, annualized spend, confidence, owner questions, and cancellation-risk checks. A human confirms usage, contract terms, and operational dependency.
