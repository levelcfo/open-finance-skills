# Review revenue leakage
Compare completed work, approved value, change orders, invoice records, service-agreement coverage, and billing status. Report unbilled and underbilled candidates separately. Do not treat every completed job without an invoice as leakage. Return the evidence gap, possible explanations, amount at risk, and follow-up owner. Human review confirms the billing obligation.
