# Review spend concentration
Normalize vendors, aggregate a defined period, and calculate vendor, category, and top-five shares. Separate recurring spend from capital or one-time purchases. Return totals, denominators, concentration shares, trend, and supplier questions. Concentration is a signal, not automatically a problem. A finance professional interprets pricing, continuity, and operating context.
